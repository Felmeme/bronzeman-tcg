# PK Collection curation

Planning list only. Checking a box means **keep this card in the named PK Collection
subcategory**. Card names below match the current bundled card catalogue.

`Extra Food` and `Extra Potions` should not be maintained by hand. They are the
card-backed entries in `consumables.json` left after the Blighted and Commonly Used
lists are removed. This prevents duplicate assignments and places future consumable
cards somewhere safe until they are reviewed.

## Review notes

- Keep an item in only one visible subcategory within PK Collection.
- Untradeables may also naturally fit an equipment category; the dedicated
  Untradeables section should take precedence so rows are not duplicated.
- Any future card-backed food or potion begins in Extra until reviewed.
- No Card Needed rows are informational and never affect unlocked/total counts.
